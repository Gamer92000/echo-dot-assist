✅ works, tried on one Echo of that model · 🚧 being worked on · ❌ not supported

A port starts with an unlock. Only ✅ and 🚧 have a public one that the wiki or this project knows of, so work on the
others cannot start yet. Vega OS is Amazon's newer system, not Android, and has not been looked at here.

Names, model numbers, codenames and dates come from the
[Amazon device codenames](https://bitbyte.miraheze.org/wiki/Amazon_device_codenames) wiki as of 2026-11-01, which has no
Echo Buds, Frames or Loop.
